Privacy Policy for Instant AR App

This app uses your device's camera for augmented reality (AR) experiences, such as displaying 3D products in your environment.

The camera is used only for AR visualization and no images or videos are recorded, saved, or transmitted.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from your device.

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us at support@krishashukla.com.
